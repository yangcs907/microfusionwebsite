Services

MEL provides automation migration projects on a full-scope turnkey basis or as an application services provider. The scope can include full design and implementation of the control system and instrumentation control system checkout and training simulator and services, and complete project management. Digital Twins/Simulators

MEL offers the most cost-effective solutions in the simulation industry. MEL's Thermal Hydraulic Integrated Network (THiNK™) models assure control system quality by testing the control logic, operator graphics, alarms, interlocks, and sequences against a realistic model of the process before startup. By connecting the control system to a simulator, engineers can validate how the system responds to normal operations, disturbances, equipment failures, and abnormal conditions without risking plant assets or personnel.

THiNK simulator training provides a realistic, hands-on environment where operators can practice responding to normal operations, upsets, startups, shutdowns, and emergency events without risk to the plant.

Minor equipment failures can create complex chain reactions that lead to equipment damage, operational disruption, and costly downtime. MEL simulations help operators practice responses to equipment failures, process disturbances, and abnormal plant conditions.

verified NERC Validation
